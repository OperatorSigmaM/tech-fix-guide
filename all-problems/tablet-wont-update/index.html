Tablet Won't Update? 12 Ways to Fix Software Update Failures

📅 Updated: January 2025 ⏱️ 13 min read Easy

🎯 Quick Summary

Main causes: Insufficient storage (35%), poor WiFi connection (30%), low battery (15%), server issues (10%), software corruption (10%)

Quick fixes: Free up storage, connect to stable WiFi, charge to 50%+, restart tablet, delete and re-download update

Success rate: 90% fixable with storage cleanup and connection fixes, 8% need computer update, 2% require professional help

  • Identify Update Problem

First: Identify Your Update Failure

Different error types:

🔴 "Not Enough Storage" or "Insufficient Storage":

  • Download starts but fails midway
  • Error says need X GB free space
  • Cause: Update needs temporary space (often 2x update size)
  • Solution: Free up storage (see Solution #1)

🔴 "Unable to Check for Update" or won't connect:

  • Can't even check if update available
  • Spinning wheel forever
  • "Unable to connect to server"
  • Cause: WiFi issue, Apple/Google servers down
  • Solution: Check WiFi, wait and retry

🔴 Download stuck at percentage (50%, 90%, etc.):

  • Download progress bar frozen
  • Doesn't move for hours
  • Cause: Network interruption, server congestion
  • Solution: Delete and restart download

🔴 "Update Failed" after installation:

  • Downloads successfully
  • Installation starts but fails
  • Tablet restarts to previous version
  • Cause: Corrupted download, software conflict
  • Solution: Delete update, re-download

🔴 "Your Device is Up to Date" (but it's not):

  • Says up to date but you know newer version exists
  • Cause: Device too old for latest update, region restriction
  • Solution: Check device compatibility
💡 Update size reference:
• iPad/iPhone: Major iOS updates 3-6GB
• Android tablets: Major updates 1-3GB
• Required free space: Usually 2x update size (temporary files)
• Example: 3GB update needs 6GB+ free space

Immediate Update Fixes

1 Free Up Storage Space Success: 45%

Why it works: #1 reason for update failures. Updates need significant temporary storage.

Check available storage:

  • iPad: Settings → General → iPad Storage
  • Android: Settings → Storage
  • See total available space

How much space needed:

  • Major iOS update (17→18): 6-8GB free minimum
  • Minor iOS update: 3-5GB free
  • Android major update: 4-6GB free
  • Android minor update: 2-3GB free

Quick storage cleanup:

  1. Delete unused apps:
    • Settings → General → iPad Storage (iPad)
    • Tap apps you don't use → Delete App
    • Games often 1-5GB each
  2. Offload apps (iPad):
    • Same menu → Offload App
    • Removes app but keeps data
    • Can reinstall later
  3. Delete photos/videos:
    • Back up to iCloud/Google Photos first
    • Delete from device
    • Empty Recently Deleted folder
  4. Clear Safari/Chrome cache:
    • Settings → Safari → Clear History and Data (iPad)
    • Settings → Apps → Chrome → Storage → Clear Cache (Android)
  5. Delete old messages (iPad):
    • Settings → Messages → Keep Messages → 30 Days
    • Frees up GB if you have years of messages
✓ Target: Get to at least 10GB free for major updates, 5GB for minor updates. More is better!

2 Connect to Strong WiFi Success: 35%

Why it matters: Weak or unstable WiFi causes download interruptions and failures.

WiFi requirements:

  • Strong, stable connection (3+ bars)
  • Not cellular data (updates require WiFi)
  • Download speed 10+ Mbps recommended
  • Unlimited data (updates are large)

Improve WiFi connection:

  1. Move closer to WiFi router (same room if possible)
  2. Disconnect other devices using bandwidth (Netflix, gaming)
  3. Restart WiFi router:
    • Unplug router
    • Wait 30 seconds
    • Plug back in
    • Wait 2 minutes to fully boot
  4. Forget and rejoin WiFi network:
    • Settings → WiFi → (i) next to network
    • Forget This Network
    • Reconnect and enter password
  5. Try different WiFi network (friend, library, café)
💡 Best time to update: Late night or early morning when fewer people using WiFi. Updates download faster with less network congestion.

3 Charge Battery to 50%+ Success: 20%

Why it's required: System prevents updates on low battery to avoid bricking device if power dies mid-install.

Battery requirements:

  • iPad/iPhone: Minimum 50% battery OR plugged into charger
  • Android: Usually 30-50% minimum OR charging
  • Best practice: Plug in during entire update process

Update while charging:

  1. Connect to charger (wall adapter, not computer USB)
  2. Wait until battery shows 50%+
  3. Keep plugged in during download AND installation
  4. Don't unplug even if battery shows 100%
  5. Update can take 30 minutes to 2 hours total
⚠️ Critical: NEVER unplug or let battery die during installation phase. Can corrupt OS and brick device. Always keep charging throughout entire process.

4 Restart Tablet Success: 25%

Why it works: Clears temporary glitches preventing update from starting or completing.

Normal restart:

  1. iPad: Hold Power button → Slide to power off → Wait 30 sec → Power on
  2. Android: Hold Power → Restart (or Power off → Power on)
  3. After restart, try update again

When to restart:

  • Before starting update download
  • If download stuck for 30+ minutes
  • After freeing up storage
  • If getting error messages

5 Delete Downloaded Update and Retry Success: 30%

Why it works: Corrupted download files cause installation failures. Fresh download fixes it.

iPad - Delete update:

  1. Settings → General → iPad Storage
  2. Scroll to find "iOS XX Update" (often near top)
  3. Tap it
  4. Delete Update
  5. Confirm deletion
  6. Restart iPad
  7. Settings → General → Software Update
  8. Download fresh copy

Android - Clear update cache:

  1. Settings → Apps → Show system apps
  2. Find "Software Update" or "System Update"
  3. Storage → Clear Cache
  4. Clear Data (if available)
  5. Restart tablet
  6. Try update again

6 Check if Update Actually Available Diagnostic

Why it matters: Old tablets may not support latest OS versions.

iPad compatibility (iPadOS 18 - Jan 2025):

  • iPad Pro (2nd gen and newer - 2017+)
  • iPad Air (3rd gen and newer - 2019+)
  • iPad (6th gen and newer - 2018+)
  • iPad Mini (5th gen and newer - 2019+)

If your iPad older:

  • Won't receive latest iPadOS
  • "Your software is up to date" is accurate
  • Stuck on older iOS version (15, 16, 17)
  • Can't update further

Android tablet compatibility:

  • Varies wildly by manufacturer
  • Flagship tablets: 3-4 years of updates
  • Budget tablets: Often 1-2 years only
  • Check manufacturer website for your model

Verify latest version:

  1. Google "latest iPadOS version" or "latest Android version"
  2. Compare to your current version (Settings → General → About)
  3. If same = you're actually up to date
  4. If different = update should be available (if device compatible)

Advanced Update Solutions

7 Update During Off-Peak Hours Success: 20%

Why it helps: Apple/Google servers get overloaded when new update releases. Less traffic = faster, more reliable downloads.

Best times to update:

  • 2AM - 6AM local time (fewest users)
  • Weekday mornings (Monday-Thursday)
  • 2-3 days after update release (initial rush over)

Worst times:

  • Day of release (first 24 hours)
  • 6PM - 11PM (peak usage)
  • Weekends (more people updating)

Schedule overnight update:

  1. Start download before bed
  2. Keep tablet plugged in and on WiFi
  3. Enable automatic installation (if option available)
  4. Wake up to updated tablet

8 Reset Network Settings Success: 15%

Why it works: Network configuration issues prevent update downloads.

iPad - Reset Network Settings:

  1. Settings → General → Transfer or Reset iPad
  2. Reset
  3. Reset Network Settings
  4. Enter passcode
  5. Confirm reset
  6. iPad restarts
  7. Reconnect to WiFi (need password)
  8. Try update again

What gets reset:

  • WiFi passwords (need to re-enter)
  • Bluetooth pairings
  • VPN settings
  • Cellular settings

What stays:

  • All apps, photos, data
  • Everything else unchanged

Update via Computer (Most Reliable)

9 Update iPad via Mac/PC Success: 85%

Why it works: Bypasses WiFi issues, uses computer's stable internet connection. Most reliable method.

Requirements:

  • Mac with macOS Catalina+ OR PC with iTunes
  • Lightning or USB-C cable
  • Computer connected to internet
  • 10GB+ free space on computer

Update via Mac (Finder):

  1. Connect iPad to Mac with cable
  2. Open Finder
  3. Click iPad in sidebar (under Locations)
  4. Click "General" tab
  5. Click "Check for Update"
  6. If update available, click "Download and Update"
  7. Enter iPad passcode if prompted
  8. Wait for download (10-30 minutes)
  9. Installation starts automatically
  10. Keep connected until complete (30-60 minutes total)

Update via Windows PC (iTunes):

  1. Download iTunes from Microsoft Store or apple.com
  2. Connect iPad to PC with cable
  3. Open iTunes
  4. Click iPad icon (top left)
  5. Click "Summary" in left sidebar
  6. Click "Check for Update"
  7. Click "Download and Update"
  8. Follow same steps as Mac
✓ Advantages: More reliable than WiFi, faster download via ethernet, works when tablet WiFi broken, can recover from errors easier.
⚠️ During update: Don't disconnect cable, don't close Finder/iTunes, don't let computer sleep. Keep iPad connected entire time.

10 Update Android via Computer (varies by brand) Success: 70%

Android updates more complex - depends on manufacturer:

Samsung Galaxy Tab:

  1. Download Samsung Smart Switch on PC/Mac
  2. Connect tablet via USB
  3. Open Smart Switch
  4. Click "More" → "Emergency software recovery and initialization"
  5. Or: "Software update" if available
  6. Follow prompts

Google Pixel Tablet:

  1. Download Android Flash Tool or OTA update
  2. Enable Developer Options → USB Debugging
  3. Connect to PC
  4. Use Android SDK tools to sideload update
  5. (Advanced - requires technical knowledge)

Other Android tablets:

  • Check manufacturer website for PC update tools
  • Lenovo, Asus, Amazon have proprietary software
  • Some require factory reset via Recovery Mode

11 Factory Reset Then Update Success: 60%

⚠️ Last resort - ERASES EVERYTHING!

When to consider:

  • All other methods failed
  • Software severely corrupted
  • Have complete backup
  • Willing to set up as new device

Why it helps:

  • Clears corrupted files blocking update
  • Frees maximum storage space
  • Fresh system more likely to update successfully

Process:

  1. BACKUP EVERYTHING FIRST!
    • iCloud/Google backup
    • Export photos to computer
    • Save important files
  2. Factory reset device (Settings → Reset)
  3. Set up as new (don't restore backup yet)
  4. Immediately try updating
  5. After update succeeds, restore backup

12 Contact Apple/Manufacturer Support Professional Help

When to contact support:

  • All DIY solutions failed
  • Getting specific error codes
  • Update critical for security/compatibility
  • Device under warranty

Apple Support for iPad:

  1. Visit support.apple.com
  2. Select iPad → Software Update issue
  3. Chat, phone, or Genius Bar appointment
  4. Often can diagnose remotely
  5. May need to bring to Apple Store

Android Manufacturer Support:

  • Samsung: samsung.com/support
  • Google: support.google.com
  • Others: Check manufacturer website

Common error codes:

  • iOS error 4000, 4013, 4014: Connection issue - try different cable/USB port
  • iOS error 9: Computer issue - update iTunes, restart computer
  • "Unable to verify update": Date/time wrong - set automatically
  • "Update Requested": Server busy - wait and try later

Why Updates Fail - Common Reasons

1. Storage Issues (35% of failures)

  • Not enough free space for temporary files
  • Update needs 2x its size for extraction
  • Hidden "Other" storage taking space
  • Solution: Free up 10GB+ minimum

2. Network Problems (30%)

  • Weak WiFi signal causing interruptions
  • Router issues or ISP problems
  • Server overload (especially day of release)
  • Solution: Better WiFi, update via computer, try different network

3. Battery/Power (15%)

  • Battery too low (under 50%)
  • Not plugged into charger
  • Power interruption during install
  • Solution: Charge to 50%+, keep plugged in entire time

4. Software Corruption (10%)

  • Previous update failed partially
  • System files corrupted
  • Download corrupted
  • Solution: Delete update and re-download, factory reset

5. Device Too Old (10%)

  • Tablet no longer supported
  • Reached end of update lifecycle
  • Solution: None - device at maximum OS version

Prevention: Avoid Future Update Problems

1. Maintain Free Storage

  • Keep 15-20% storage free at all times
  • Regularly delete unused apps
  • Use cloud storage for photos (iCloud, Google Photos)
  • Clear cache monthly

2. Update Regularly

  • Don't skip multiple versions
  • Install updates when they become available
  • Jumping many versions causes more problems
  • Enable automatic updates if comfortable

3. Backup Before Updating

  • Always backup before major updates
  • iCloud or computer backup
  • If update fails badly, can restore

4. Good Update Habits

  • Update overnight when not using device
  • Connect to charger
  • Use stable WiFi (home, not public)
  • Don't interrupt update process
  • Wait 2-3 days after release for bug fixes

Common Questions

Q: How long should an iPad/tablet update take?

A: Download: 20-60 minutes (depends on WiFi speed and update size). Installation: 15-30 minutes. Total: 1-2 hours typical. If stuck longer than 3 hours, something's wrong.

Q: Can I use my tablet while update is downloading?

A: Yes during download phase (but will be slower). NO during installation - don't touch it, let it complete. Screen will be black or show progress bar during install.

Q: What if update says "Preparing Update" for hours?

A: If stuck over 1 hour at "Preparing", force restart device. Delete downloaded update, free up more storage, try again. May need to update via computer.

Q: Should I update to the latest version right away?

A: Best practice: Wait 3-5 days after release. Lets Apple/Google fix initial bugs. Read reviews to see if update is stable. Security updates: Install immediately.

Q: Why does iPad say "Update Requested" and nothing happens?

A: Apple servers queuing your update. Usually resolves within 24 hours. Try again in few hours, overnight, or next day. If persists 48+ hours, delete and retry.

Q: Can a failed update damage my tablet?

A: Rarely, but possible if power dies during installation. That's why must stay plugged in. If truly bricked, can usually restore via computer in Recovery Mode.

Q: My tablet is old (5+ years). Should I update?

A: If update available, yes for security. But know that newer OS may slow down old hardware. Read reviews from users with your tablet model first.

Q: What's "Other" storage and how do I delete it to free space for updates?

A: System cache and temporary files. To clear: Reset All Settings (doesn't delete data), or factory reset. Can also try deleting and reinstalling large apps.

Q: Can I downgrade if update causes problems?

A: iPad: No official way to downgrade iOS. Android: Sometimes possible but requires technical knowledge and unlocked bootloader. Better to research update before installing.

Q: Why is the update file so large (3-6GB)?

A: Contains entire new OS version. Major updates replace system files. Security updates smaller (100-500MB). That's why need lots of free space.
